Scry

A crystal-ball skin for Video.js v10.

Drag the timeline, or drag sideways on the picture, and the video curls into a crystal ball. Hold still and the vision comes into focus. Let go and step into it.

Time travel

Scrub or jump and the picture wraps into a glass orb, with the timeline curled around its rim. Inside it the video flows from one moment to the next instead of cutting.

On your device

Optical flow, the morph and the lens are fragment shaders running in your browser, with no model to download. Resting on a moment fetches that one frame from the video's own host.

For everyone

Every control works from the keyboard and on touch. With reduced motion on, nothing wraps: seeking is instant and the preview is a still frame.
