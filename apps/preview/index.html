Scry

A crystal-ball skin for Video.js v10.

Move along the timeline to look ahead. Hold still and the vision comes into focus. Let go and step into it.

Look ahead

The seek preview is a glass orb that rises from the playhead. Inside it the video flows from one moment to the next instead of flipping between thumbnails.

On your device

Optical flow, the morph and the lens are fragment shaders running in your browser, with no model to download. Resting on a moment fetches that one frame from the video's own host.

For everyone

Every control works from the keyboard and on touch. With reduced motion on, the orb holds a still frame and seeking is instant.
