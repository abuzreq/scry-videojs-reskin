Scry

A Video.js skin that turns the video into a seeing-stone.

Press play, then drag the timeline or the picture.

Things to try

Drag
The timeline, or the picture itself. The video rolls into the stone and plays through it.
Hover
Rest on the timeline for a look ahead.
Skip
Arrow keys, or double-click a side. Long skips pass through the stone.
Wait
Smoke while it loads. At the end, the first frame again.

How it works

Every video comes with a storyboard: one image with a small frame every few seconds, meant for thumbnails. Scry runs optical flow over it, estimating how each point of the picture moves from one frame to the next, then warps and blends the frames along that motion, so a few dozen stills play like footage. The flow, the blend, the glass and the fire are all WebGL shaders running in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Underneath are the standard Video.js controls, keyboard and screen reader support included. With reduced motion turned on, the stone stays away.
